Explanation depth

Every question teaches you the trap.

Not a paragraph — a diagnosis. Here's a real one, on the decline of Taxila.

"Taxila's decline closely paralleled the decline of the Gupta Empire, with both occurring as a direct result of Bakhtiyar Khilji's campaigns." — Correct or incorrect?

The trap · Temporal swap Sounds plausible if you memorised "Gupta decline → educational decline." But Taxila fell nearly six centuries before Khilji.
Elimination logic Khilji attacked Bihar — Nalanda, Odantapuri, Vikramashila. Never Taxila. Eliminate instantly.
Value addition The real blow: White Hun invasions, 5th–6th century CE. Unlike Nalanda, Taxila never saw a Pala-era revival.
