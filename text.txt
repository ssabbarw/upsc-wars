# UPSC Wars Home Screen & Retention Discussion

I want your product critique, especially around retention and home screen design.

## Context

UPSC Wars is a UPSC preparation app.

Current DAU: ~72

Current MAU: ~1.2K

Total users (Firebase): ~3,878

Average engagement time: ~18 minutes

Engaged sessions/user: ~2.7

Play Store conversion: ~31% (store visitors → acquisitions)

D1 retention appears around ~20%.

D7 retention appears roughly ~8-10%.

Users who engage appear reasonably engaged, but retention is not where I want it to be.

---

## Core Differentiator

Most UPSC apps only track right/wrong.

UPSC Wars also asks users how confident they were before answering:

* Sure
* Educated Guess
* Pure Guess

The app then builds:

* Confidence analysis
* Trap awareness
* Weak topic detection
* Revision recommendations
* Subject insights

Example:

A user marks "Sure" but gets the answer wrong.

The app treats this as more dangerous than a normal wrong answer.

## Current Result Screen

After a test, users see:

* Score
* Confidence analysis
* Revision queue
* Progress graphs
* Detailed breakdowns

The problem we identified:

The most differentiated insights exist, but are buried below the fold or mixed with other information.

---

## Main Product Concern

My onboarding heavily talks about:

* Confidence tracking
* Hidden mistakes
* Better self-awareness

But the home screen mostly feels like a traditional subject-wise MCQ app.

Potential mismatch:

Onboarding promise:
"We'll show you mistakes other apps miss."

Home screen reality:
"Choose a subject."

---

## Home Screen Direction Being Considered

### Recovery Plan becomes a major feature

Not necessarily the only feature.

But a highly visible section near the top.

The goal:

Surface the unique value of UPSC Wars every day.

---

### Proposed Recovery Plan

Instead of one mixed queue, we discussed multiple cards.

#### Card 1: Current Focus

Review 6 confident mistakes from Polity.

Why?

Because the user has recently spent most study time in Polity.

---

#### Card 2: Due For Revision

Review 4 mistakes from Medieval History.

Why?

These mistakes have not been revisited for a long time.

---

Potential future card:

#### Weak Area

Practice questions from a topic where the user's accuracy is poor.

---

## Current Debate

I am unsure about the actual algorithm behind Recovery Plan.

One concern:

If a user is currently studying Polity, they may not appreciate seeing Ancient History questions from two months ago.

Another concern:

If Recovery Plan only follows recent subjects, it may ignore important weak areas.

We discussed:

* Current focus subjects
* Confident mistakes
* Weak topics
* Long-overdue revision

But have not finalized the balance.

---

## New Users

We also discussed how Recovery Plan should appear for first-time users.

Ideas considered:

1. Interactive onboarding
2. Discovery quiz
3. Unlock insights after enough questions
4. Guided "Today's Plan"

We rejected the idea of generating deep insights from only 5 questions because the sample size is too small to be meaningful.

---

## Current Home Screen Concept

Top:

Good Morning
85 Days to Prelims

Then:

Recovery Plan (primary)

Card carousel:

* Current Focus
* Due For Revision
* Weak Area

Then:

Subjects

The last studied subject gets a larger card.

Example:

Polity
Last practiced 2 hours ago

Other subjects remain normal-sized cards.

Potentially remove:

* Daily goals
* Streaks
* Motivational quote

unless they are shown to provide clear retention value.
